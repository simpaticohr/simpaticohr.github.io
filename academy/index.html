Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about admissions, syllabus, fees, and career placement.

Who is eligible to join Simpatico HR Academy?
Any graduate is eligible to enroll — including graduates of BBA, MBA, MSW, B.Com, BA, B.Sc, B.Tech, or equivalent streams. The curriculum starts from the fundamentals of recruitment and statutory compliance, progressing to advanced AI-powered HR operations, making it suitable for both freshers and working professionals seeking career transitions.
What makes Simpatico HR Academy different from other HR institutes?
Simpatico HR Academy is part of an active corporate HR consultancy firm. You don't just learn hypothetical theory; you work on active job openings, utilize real applicant tracking systems (ATS), practice with our proprietary Gemini 3.8 AI speech simulator, calculate dual-jurisdiction payrolls (India Budget 2024 & UAE WPS), and receive guaranteed 1-month corporate internship experience.
How does the 100% live online class work for Kochi, Calicut, or Gulf students?
Online students receive the exact same 1-on-1 personalized faculty mentoring through scheduled live video sessions. You receive immediate login access to our Student LMS (student-portal.html) featuring interactive payroll calculators, AI interview simulators, and digital course toolkits, accompanied by remote internship placement.
What are the course fees and payment installment options?
The complete 3-month International HRM Diploma begins from ₹15,000 with transparent pricing and no hidden costs. Flexible 2-part and 3-part installment plans as well as zero-interest EMI options are available. International candidates can pay in AED, USD, EUR, or GBP.
Does the academy guarantee placement assistance?
Yes, 100% placement support is provided to all certified graduates. This includes one-on-one resume transformation with ATS keyword optimization, LinkedIn profile optimization, corporate mock interview rehearsals, and direct referrals across our network of 500+ corporate hiring partners throughout Kerala, Bangalore, and GCC countries.
