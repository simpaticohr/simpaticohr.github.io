Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on Offline & Live Online Academy batches, HR SaaS pricing, consulting, and recruitment.

Does Simpatico HR Academy offer both offline and online HR classes?
Yes. You can attend Offline classroom sessions at our Perinthalmanna campus (SMS Arcade, First Floor, Opp GMHSS, Kerala 679322) OR join 100% Live Interactive 1-on-1 Online classes from Kochi, Calicut, Trivandrum, anywhere in India, or the GCC. Both modes include identical live mentors, India + UAE payroll labs, and a guaranteed internship starting from ₹15,000.
What courses and duration are available at Simpatico HR Academy?
We offer 7 practical programs from 15 days to 12 months: International HRM Certification (3-month fast track & 5-month executive), Talent Acquisition Mastery (6 weeks / HR-TA-601), AI-Powered Recruitment (2 months), AI Tools for Office (2 months), Corporate Communication & Spoken English (1 month), Business Development & CRM (2 months), and our 1-Year Dual Diploma — all verifiable online at simpaticohr.in/verify.html.
Is there a free applicant tracking system (ATS) and how much does the HR software cost?
Yes — Simpatico HR offers a Free Tier (3 job posts, AI resume screening, branded careers page, Google for Jobs sync, no credit card required). Paid B2B SaaS plans start at ₹4,999/month (~$60/mo), up to 80% more affordable than Greenhouse, Workday, and Lever.
Does Simpatico HR offer business consultancy, corporate wellness, and DEI programs?
Yes. Alongside our HR SaaS platform and Academy, we operate dedicated divisions for Business & Entrepreneurship Consulting, HR Consulting & Women in Leadership (DEI), Non-Clinical Corporate Wellness & Mentoring, and Global Influencer Marketing.
Which regions do you serve and do you provide visa sponsorship?
We support B2B clients, consulting engagements, and live online academy trainees across 13+ countries including India, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, UK, USA, Germany, Canada, and Australia. For recruitment placements, we specialize in local talent sourcing and do not provide visa processing or visa sponsorship.
