**Job Title: Recruitment Executive** **Join Our Dynamic HR Team and Shape the Future of Talent Acquisition** We're on a mission to build a diverse, inclusive, and high-performing team that reflects the best of our organization. As a Recruitment Executive, you'll play a pivotal role in identifying, attracting, and hiring top talent from diverse backgrounds. If you're passionate about people, driven by results, and committed to excellence, we want to hear from you. **Responsibilities:** - Source, attract, and engage top talent from diverse backgrounds through various channels, including social media, job boards, and professional networks. - Develop and implement effective recruitment strategies to meet business needs and goals. - Collaborate with hiring managers to understand their requirements and provide personalized support throughout the recruitment process. - Conduct interviews, assessments, and reference checks to ensure the best fit for our organization. - Maintain accurate and up-to-date records of recruitment activities, including candidate interactions and outcomes. - Stay up-to-date with industry trends, best practices, and regulatory requirements to ensure compliance and excellence in recruitment. - Contribute to the development of a positive employer brand and employee value proposition. **Requirements:** **Must-Haves:** - 0-1 years of experience in recruitment or a related field. - Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to build rapport with diverse stakeholders. - Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel, Word, and PowerPoint. - Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and prioritize multiple tasks and deadlines. **Nice-to-Haves:** - Experience with recruitment software and systems, such as applicant tracking systems (ATS). - Knowledge of employment law and regulations, including equal employment opportunity (EEO) and diversity and inclusion (D&I) best practices. - Certification in recruitment or a related field, such as SHRM-CP or HRCI. **Benefits:** - Competitive salary and benefits package. - Opportunities for professional growth and development, including training and mentorship. - Collaborative and dynamic work environment with a diverse team of professionals. - Flexible work arrangements, including remote work options. - Access to our employee assistance program (EAP) and wellness initiatives. **Diversity Statement:** We're committed to creating an inclusive and diverse workplace where everyone feels valued, respected, and empowered to succeed. We believe that diversity is a strength, and we're dedicated to building a team that reflects the best of our organization.
