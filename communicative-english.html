        Frequently Asked Questions

        What is the Communicative English AI Coach? +
        The Communicative English AI Coach is a free, AI-powered spoken English practice platform by Simpatico HR. It uses Google Gemini's Live API for full-duplex (real-time two-way) voice conversation — meaning you speak naturally and the AI responds instantly, just like a human tutor. It offers 8 practice modes, real-time scoring across 5 metrics, CEFR level estimation (A1–C2), downloadable certificates, and a structured 8-week learning path.
        Is this free? What do I need to use it? +
        Yes, it is completely free. You need: (1) a Simpatico HR Career Lab account, (2) a Google Gemini API key (also free from aistudio.google.com/apikey), and (3) Chrome or Edge browser for microphone + speech recognition support. There are no subscriptions, usage limits, or hidden fees.
        How is this different from ELSA Speak, Cambly, or Duolingo? +
        vs ELSA Speak ($50/yr): ELSA uses record-and-score; we use full-duplex real-time voice. vs Cambly ($100+/yr): Cambly uses human tutors; we use AI available 24/7 for free. vs Duolingo: Duolingo is text/tap-based; we offer real spoken conversation practice. Our unique advantage: full-duplex AI voice + 8 specialized modes + live scoring + gamification — all free.
        Can I use this for IELTS Speaking test preparation? +
        Yes! The IELTS Speaking Test mode simulates all 3 parts of the real IELTS exam — Part 1 (Introduction), Part 2 (Cue Card), and Part 3 (Discussion). The AI acts as a certified IELTS examiner, asks realistic questions, and provides estimated band scores. You can practice unlimited times with no cost.
        What are the 8 practice modes? +
        (1) Free Conversation — Open chat on any topic. (2) Workplace English — Meetings, interviews, client calls. (3) IELTS Speaking Test — Full exam simulation. (4) Pronunciation Drills — Difficult sounds, minimal pairs. (5) Grammar Coach — Real-time grammar corrections. (6) Debate & Discussion — AI takes the opposing stance. (7) Role-Play — Restaurant, airport, doctor scenarios. (8) Presentation Practice — Structure, clarity, filler scoring.
        How does the scoring work? +
        You are scored in real-time across 5 metrics: Fluency (words per minute, pause patterns), Grammar (error-based deductions from AI corrections), Pronunciation (browser SpeechRecognition confidence scores), Vocabulary (unique word ratio + lexical diversity), and Confidence (filler word frequency). After each session, you receive a CEFR level estimate (A1–C2) and can download a certificate.
        What browsers are supported? +
        Chrome and Microsoft Edge on desktop/laptop are recommended. The full-duplex voice engine works on any modern browser with WebSocket support, but the SpeechRecognition API (used for pronunciation scoring and transcript generation) is currently only available in Chromium-based browsers.
