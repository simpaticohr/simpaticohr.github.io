Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Simpatico for your next creator campaign.

What regions and languages are covered?

We execute campaigns across India (English, Hindi, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali), GCC & Middle East (Dubai/UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar with native Arabic and English creators), UK, USA, Germany, France, and Southeast Asia (Singapore, Malaysia).

How do you guarantee follower quality and prevent bot fraud?

Every creator passes our automated AI audit analyzing audience authenticity, comment sentiment, follower velocity anomalies, and historical engagement consistency. We guarantee zero fake engagement on campaign rosters.

Can we use creator content for our own paid ads?

Yes. All Simpatico campaigns include standard or optional extended digital usage rights, creator whitelisting (dark posting), and high-resolution raw assets for Meta, TikTok, and YouTube ad boosts.

How does B2B influencer marketing work?

B2B influencer marketing focuses on trust and peer authority rather than viral dances. We partner with respected LinkedIn creators, industry newsletter writers, podcast hosts, and certified professionals who review your software, co-host webinars, or author analytical case studies.

What is Employer Branding via Influencer Marketing?

It is the practice of engaging industry-specific career mentors, tech creators, and employees to create authentic behind-the-scenes content that showcases your company culture, attracting elite talent while slashing job-board recruitment ad spending.

What is the minimum campaign budget?

Pilot starter packages begin at $1,500 (or ₹99,000 in India). We scale up to full-service multi-month retainer campaigns for multi-national consumer brands and tech enterprises with guaranteed deliverables.
