5 Affordable Greenhouse Alternatives That Actually Work in 2026

Why pay $6,500+/month for Greenhouse when modern AI ATS platforms offer superior features at a fraction of the cost?

The Problem with Greenhouse Pricing

Greenhouse is widely recognized in enterprise recruitment, but its pricing structure creates significant friction for growing companies:

  • Minimum annual contracts starting upwards of $6,500 to $10,000+ per year.
  • No free tier or trial option for small hiring teams.
  • Additional fees for AI screening features and custom integrations.

Top 5 Greenhouse Alternatives

  1. Simpatico HR: The #1 budget alternative. Offers AI resume screening, proctored AI video interviews, and payroll — 80% cheaper with a free tier.
  2. Workable: Flexible month-to-month plans starting at $189/mo.
  3. JazzHR: Simple ATS starting at $75/mo (though lacks AI video features).
  4. Zoho Recruit: Good entry-level option for small teams using Zoho products.
  5. Freshteam: User-friendly software with basic free tier for small teams.
