Demystifying HR software pricing models, hidden implementation fees, and how to get maximum ROI on your HR tech investment.

Overview of HR Software Pricing Models

HR software pricing can be complex and opaque. Most vendors use one of three main pricing models:

  • Per-Employee Per-Month (PEPM): Ranging from $4 to $15+ per employee each month (e.g., BambooHR, Workday). As your headcount grows, costs explode.
  • Enterprise Tiered Pricing: Ranging from $3,500 to $10,000+ per month regardless of headcount (e.g., Greenhouse, Lever).
  • Flat Monthly / Transparent SaaS: Flat fee starting from $0 to $60/month with unlimited or high-tier feature access (e.g., Simpatico HR).

Hidden Costs to Watch Out For

When evaluating HR vendors, watch out for these commonly hidden line items:

  1. Implementation & Onboarding Fees: Often range from $1,000 to $15,000 upfront.
  2. AI Feature Add-ons: Charging extra for resume parsing, AI candidate scoring, or video interviewing.
  3. Multi-Region Compliance: Extra fees for international tax/payroll modules.
