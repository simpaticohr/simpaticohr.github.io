10 Best Affordable HR Software in 2026 — Complete Comparison & Pricing

2. Zoho People — Best for Zoho Ecosystem Users

Zoho People

From $1.25/employee/month | Free plan for 5 employees

Zoho People is a solid budget HR option if you're already in the Zoho ecosystem. It integrates well with Zoho Recruit, Zoho Payroll, and Zoho CRM. However, you'll need multiple Zoho subscriptions to match the features Simpatico HR offers in one platform.

Pros:
  • Very low per-employee pricing
  • Good Zoho ecosystem integration
  • Decent leave and attendance tracking
Cons:
  • No AI video interviews
  • ATS requires separate Zoho Recruit subscription
  • Payroll requires separate Zoho Payroll subscription

3. Freshteam by Freshworks — Best Free ATS for Beginners

Freshteam

From $1.20/employee/month | Free plan available

Freshteam offers a decent free plan with basic ATS capabilities. Good for companies just getting started with recruitment automation. However, advanced features and AI capabilities are limited compared to newer platforms.

Pros:
  • Free plan with basic ATS
  • Clean, modern interface
  • Good onboarding workflows
Cons:
  • No AI resume screening in free plan
  • No video interview capability
  • Limited analytics

4. Bitrix24 — Best Free All-in-One (with limitations)

Bitrix24

Free plan available | Paid from $49/month (5 users)

Bitrix24 is primarily a CRM and project management tool with HR features added on. The free plan is generous but the HR module is basic. Good if you need HR + CRM + project management in one tool on a tight budget.

5. OrangeHRM — Best Open-Source Option

OrangeHRM

Free community edition | Paid from $6/employee/month

OrangeHRM's open-source community edition gives you basic HR management for free — if you're comfortable with self-hosting. The paid cloud version is affordable but lacks AI features and modern recruitment tools.

6. Homebase — Best Free Scheduling Tool

Homebase

Free for 1 location | Paid from $20/location/month

Homebase excels at scheduling and time tracking for hourly workers. The free plan is generous for single-location businesses. However, it's not a full HR management platform — no ATS, no payroll processing, no performance reviews.

7. Deel — Best for Global Payroll

Deel

From $49/contractor/month | EOR from $599/employee/month

Deel is the leader in global payroll and contractor management. Excellent for distributed teams, but expensive for full HR management. Best used alongside an ATS like Simpatico HR rather than as a standalone HR platform.

8. Gusto — Best US-Focused Payroll

Gusto

From $40/month + $6/person/month

Gusto is excellent for US-based payroll with benefits administration. User-friendly and well-designed. However, it's US-only, has no ATS functionality, and gets expensive as you scale — $340/month for 50 employees.

9. JazzHR — Budget ATS

JazzHR

From $75/month

JazzHR is a simple, affordable ATS focused on small businesses. Good job posting and candidate management, but no AI features, no payroll, no HR management. You'll need additional tools to build a complete HR stack.

10. BreezyHR — ATS with Limited Free Plan

BreezyHR

Free plan (1 position) | Paid from $157/month

BreezyHR offers a modern ATS with a free plan limited to 1 active position. The paid plans jump significantly in price. Good ATS features but no payroll, no AI interviews, and limited HR management capabilities.
