Small businesses in India face unique recruitment hurdles. An ideal Applicant Tracking System (ATS) for Indian SMEs must offer:

  • Indian Statutory Compliance: Smooth integration with PF, ESI, Professional Tax, and Indian labor standards.
  • AI Resume Screening: Parsing high volumes of applications instantly.
  • Multi-Channel Syndication: Automated posting to Google for Jobs and local candidate pools.
