Any graduate or final-year student can apply — MBA HR, BBA, MSW, B.Com, BA, or equivalent. Career changers and freshers looking to enter HR are also welcome. No prior HR experience is required.

The standard HR internship is 2-3 months. Flexible schedules available for students (part-time options). The program includes a structured curriculum, weekly mentoring sessions, and a final project for certification.
