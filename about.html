Who We Are

Simpatico HR Consultancy is a full-service business consultancy, AI-powered HR technology company, and professional training academy headquartered in Perinthalmanna, Kerala, India. We combine strategic business advisory with cutting-edge AI technology to deliver integrated HR solutions, recruitment process outsourcing, business consulting, and career development services to organizations and individuals across India, the Middle East, UK, Europe, USA, Canada, Australia, and beyond.

Simpatico HR Professional Academy is the training & education division of Simpatico HR Consultancy — not a separate organization. Both operate from the same office at SMS Arcade, First Floor, Opp GMHSS, Perinthalmanna, Kerala 679322. Academy students train alongside active consultancy professionals and work on real client mandates, giving them hands-on industry exposure that traditional institutes cannot offer.

Our Founder

Faisal K

Founder & Managing Director

With an MBA in Human Resource Management and years of experience in global recruitment, HR consulting, and business advisory, Faisal K founded Simpatico HR Consultancy to bridge the gap between world-class HR technology and affordable pricing. Under his leadership, Simpatico HR has grown to serve 500+ companies across 13+ countries, launched an AI-powered SaaS platform, and established Kerala's #1 rated professional HR academy — all operating from a single, integrated office in Perinthalmanna.
