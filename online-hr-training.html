Can I take this HR course 100% online from anywhere in the world?
Yes! Our program is completely online. You will attend live virtual mentoring sessions with experienced HR instructors and access our 24/7 student portal containing flashcards, recorded lectures, compliance templates, and AI voice simulation drills. Students enroll from Europe, UK, Middle East, USA, Canada, Australia, and across Asia.
Is this program aligned with SHRM and CIPD standards?
Yes. The International HRM Professional Program is mapped directly to international competency benchmarks aligned with both SHRM and CIPD bodies of knowledge, ensuring global recognition.
What time zones are the live mentoring sessions scheduled in?
Because our coaching is delivered 1-on-1, sessions are flexibly scheduled to suit your local timezone — whether you are located in London (GMT), Berlin/Paris (CET), Dubai/Riyadh (GST/AST), New York (EST), or California (PST).

Following the training modules, all students participate in a 1-month virtual corporate internship working on live talent acquisition, resume screening, and HR policy projects with Simpatico HR Consultancy. You receive a dedicated Internship Experience Certificate upon completion.
How do I enroll and what payment methods are supported?
You can enroll directly through our secure online portal at student-enrollment.html. We accept international credit/debit cards, bank wire transfers, and multi-currency payments in EUR, GBP, USD, AED, and INR. Flexible installment options are available.
