About Simpatico Wellness — Corporate Wellness Redefined

Simpatico Wellness is the dedicated corporate wellness division of Simpatico HR Consultancy, headquartered in Perinthalmanna, Kerala, India. We provide end-to-end employee wellbeing solutions for organizations of all sizes — from startups to enterprises — across 13+ countries including India, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, UK, USA, and Europe.

Our wellness programs cover six core areas: (1) Employee Wellness Programs — mental health counseling, stress management, yoga & mindfulness, resilience training, and work-life balance coaching; (2) Employee Assistance Programs (EAP) — confidential 24/7 counseling for anxiety, depression, substance abuse, family conflicts, and financial stress; (3) Corporate Wellness Consulting — needs assessment, wellness policy design, program implementation, wellness audits, and ROI tracking; (4) Wellness Technology Platform — AI-powered mood tracking, health dashboards, anonymous pulse surveys, and engagement analytics; (5) Occupational Health & Safety — workplace safety audits, ergonomics, risk assessments, and multi-jurisdiction compliance; (6) Nutrition, Fitness & Preventive Health — corporate nutrition, fitness challenges, health screenings, and chronic disease management.

What makes Simpatico Wellness unique is our integration with Simpatico HR's broader ecosystem. Clients get access to wellness programs seamlessly integrated with our AI-powered HR platform (ATS, payroll, compliance management) — making us the only provider in the market that combines HR technology, business consultancy, and corporate wellness under one roof. This integrated approach is 60-80% more affordable than engaging separate wellness vendors like Virgin Pulse, Wellable, Calm for Business, or Headspace for Work.

Companies investing in Simpatico Wellness programs typically see a 3:1 to 6:1 ROI through 25-30% reduced absenteeism, 15-40% lower employee attrition, 10-15% higher productivity, and decreased healthcare costs. Our analytics dashboards help HR leaders track and quantify these outcomes in real-time.
